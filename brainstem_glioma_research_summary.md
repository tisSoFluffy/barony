# Adult Brainstem Glioma — Research & Clinical Trial Summary

**Prepared:** 2026-06-21
**Patient context:** 67-year-old female, newly diagnosed adult brainstem glioma; molecular testing pending at Mayo Clinic.

> **⚠️ Disclaimer:** This document is an informational synthesis of published medical literature and public clinical-trial registries (current through mid-2026). It is **not medical advice** and does not replace the judgment of the treating oncology / neuro-oncology team. ---

## The two facts that frame everything

1. **The pending molecular results from Mayo are decisive.** "Adult brainstem glioma" covers tumors with very different prognoses and treatment paths. The single most important marker is **H3 K27M** status, followed by **IDH** and **MGMT**.
2. **Most research in this disease was built around *pediatric* DIPG.** Many of the most advanced therapies (especially CAR-T) carry age caps that exclude a 67-year-old. Age eligibility is flagged throughout.

---

## 1. Diagnosis & Classification (WHO 2021)

- **Diffuse Midline Glioma, H3 K27-altered (WHO Grade 4)** — most common (~50–70% of brainstem gliomas), most aggressive; Grade 4 regardless of histologic appearance. Mechanisms: H3.1/H3.3 K27M mutation, EZHIP overexpression, or EGFR mutation.
- **IDH-mutant astrocytoma** — uncommon in brainstem (~6–14% of adult cases) but substantially better prognosis.
- **IDH-wildtype glioblastoma** — behaves like hemispheric GBM.
- **Focal low-grade gliomas** (e.g., tectal, dorsally exophytic) — indolent, treated differently.

**Biopsy:** Now generally recommended in adults (vs. historical pediatric clinical-radiographic diagnosis), because the adult differential is broader (~25% imaging-vs-pathology mismatch) and molecular results gate new drugs/trials. Stereotactic brainstem biopsy: ~2.4% permanent complication rate in experienced centers, >90% diagnostic yield.

**Adult vs. pediatric DIPG:** Adults more often H3.3 (vs. pediatric H3.1) K27M; ACVR1 co-mutation rare in adults; IDH mutations present in ~10% of adults. Performance status matters more than chronological age. Some data suggest H3 K27M may not confer the same dire prognosis in older adults as in children (Frontiers Oncol 2022, PMC9214035).

---

## 2. Current Standard of Care (newly diagnosed)

1. **Biopsy + molecular profiling** — H3 K27M, IDH, MGMT, EGFR, CDKN2A.
2. **Focal radiotherapy — the cornerstone.** Standard 54–60 Gy / 30 fractions. Older/frail patients: hypofractionation (40 Gy/15 fx, or 25 Gy/5 fx) conditionally recommended (ASTRO 2025 guideline).
3. **Temozolomide** — standard in GBM, uncertain benefit in H3 K27M DMG (these tumors are nearly always MGMT-unmethylated → TMZ-resistant). Often added by extrapolation. IDH-mutant tumors more often MGMT-methylated and TMZ-responsive.
4. Surgery limited to biopsy or resection of focal/exophytic lesions; no role for radical resection in diffuse tumors.

**Prognosis (with treatment):** H3 K27M-mutant ≈ 9–15 months median OS; H3 K27M-negative adults markedly better (~40 months in one series); IDH-mutant best.

---

## 3. Major Recent Development — Dordaviprone (ONC201 / "Modeyso")

- **FDA accelerated approval, Aug 6, 2025** — first-ever approved systemic therapy for H3 K27M-mutant DMG; adults and children ≥1 yr, **recurrent/progressive disease**.
- **Mechanism:** oral, brain-penetrant; DRD2/DRD3 antagonist + mitochondrial ClpP agonist.
- **Efficacy:** ORR ~20–22%, median duration of response ~11 months; real-world first-line-after-radiation cohorts suggested median OS ~21.7 mo vs. ~12 mo historical. Well tolerated (mainly fatigue).
- **NCCN:** added as Category 2A for recurrent H3 K27M disease.
- **Newly diagnosed setting:** under study in the Phase 3 ACTION trial (below); continued approval contingent on those results.

## 5. Experimental / Emerging Therapies

- **CAR-T (GD2, B7-H3):** most dramatic responses in this disease (incl. a complete response), but currently pediatric/young-adult only — the key reason age matters.
- **H3 K27M peptide vaccine (INTERCEPT H3):** adult-focused; proof-of-concept immune responses, one durable complete response.
- **Next-gen imipridones (ONC206):** more potent ONC201 successors; Phase 1, opening to adults.
- **Combinations:** ONC201 + paxalisib (PI3K/mTOR); CDK4/6 inhibitors (ribociclib, abemaciclib) if amplified.
- **Local delivery (bypass blood-brain barrier):** convection-enhanced delivery incl. Rhenium-186 nanoliposomes (studied in adults, ReSPECT NCT01906385); focused ultrasound BBB opening (brainstem proof-of-concept).
- **Marker-driven targeted/off-label options:** avapritinib (PDGFRA), dabrafenib+trametinib (BRAF V600E), larotrectinib/entrectinib/repotrectinib (NTRK fusions, tumor-agnostic), FGFR inhibitors, osimertinib (EGFR).

## 6. What Mayo's Molecular Results Will Unlock

| If Mayo finds… | Frequency in adult brainstem glioma | Prognosis | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H3 K27M / K27-altered** | High (~50–70%) | Severe (Grade 4; ~12 mo) | Dordaviprone (approved, recurrent); ACTION/INTERCEPT/BIOMEDE trials |
| **IDH mutation** | Rare in brainstem | Favorable | Vorasidenib (approved 2024, Grade 2); IDH-inhibitor trials |
| **MGMT methylated** | Variable | Favorable | Predicts temozolomide benefit |
| **BRAF V600E** | Uncommon | Modestly favorable | Dabrafenib+trametinib (tumor-agnostic) |
| **NTRK fusion** | Rare (<1–2%) | Variable | Larotrectinib/entrectinib/repotrectinib (tumor-agnostic, approved) |
| **PDGFRA** | ~15% of HGG | Variable | Avapritinib (off-label; trials emerging) |
| **FGFR3-TACC3 / FGFR1** | ~3–4% | Slightly better | Pemigatinib, erdafitinib, infigratinib (investigational) |
| **EGFR amplification** | ~40–50% of IDH-wt GBM | Unfavorable | Investigational; osimertinib case-level |

Waiting for the full molecular profile before locking in therapy is staging, not delay — at least three approved/tumor-agnostic drug pathways and most trials are molecularly gated.

---

## 7. Bottom Line & Next Steps

- **If H3 K27M is confirmed:** the **ACTION trial (NCT05580562)** and the **INTERCEPT H3 vaccine trial (NCT04808245)** are the two most directly relevant active studies for a newly diagnosed older adult; dordaviprone is FDA-approved at recurrence.
- The CAR-T breakthroughs are real but currently **age-gated out**.
- **Action item:** bring the Mayo molecular results back for a precise, eligibility-filtered re-search and current recruiting-status check on specific NCTs.
- Useful search tools: ClinicalTrials.gov; National Brain Tumor Society trial finder (trials.braintumor.org); EU CTIS (clinicaltrialsregister.eu); dipg.org.
